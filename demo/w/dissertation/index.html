GREAT POWER WAR
AS A PROCESS_

What structural indicators of systemic conflict can, and cannot, predict

Major war read as a long structural process rather than a dated event. Two tiers ask two different questions.

SYSTEMIC TIERThe condition of the international system from 1816 to 2022, set against the build-ups to 1914 and 1939. It describes how close today's structure looks to those two moments, and attaches no probability to war.

DYADIC TIERWhether a pair of states starts a militarised dispute the following year. Trained on 1816–1989, tested on 1990–2014: split by time, never at random, so the model never sees the future it is tested on.

Every result reports base rate, AUROC, AUPR and Brier score together, against honest baselines. It incorporates its own forecasting software, the lab, built for this dissertation.

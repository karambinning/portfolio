WAR ROOMPOLYMARKET · 5-MIN CRYPTO · MAR–APRPAPER + LIVE

A self-tuning bot that bet on whether SOL, ETH, XRP, BTC or DOGE would be up or down five minutes later. Its best paper run turned $50 into $106.56 over 289 trades, but its simulator priced every entry at $0.52 and capped every loss.

On 2 April, after my first real-money order went wrong, every paper trade had to clear the live order book. At $0.52, 2 of 304 entries could fill, so I voided every earlier result.
Real money: about $55 over 162 live trades, 2–8 April. Sniping and market-making both failed on real fills: a small net loss, and I stopped in mid-April.

WAR ROOMRUN BY KRABS · 5-MIN CRYPTO · MAR–APRPAPER + LIVE

A self-tuning bot that bet on whether SOL, ETH, XRP, BTC or DOGE would be up or down five minutes later, taken through more than 100 versions in 14 days.

Method: three servers. One collected market data, one ran a long-running, controlled contest between bot strategies, and one tested new experiments. Many strategies went through it, from sniping to market-making.
Finding: sniping reached about 97.5% accuracy, but with small capital each win was tiny, and a single loss, even of only 3%, wiped out the profits. The best paper run ($50 to $106.56) was voided too: against the live order book, only 2 of 304 of its entries could fill.
What it shows: building and testing at speed, running controlled experiments, and treating my own best result with suspicion until it survives real conditions.

What it does
  • Lives in the notch. Closed, it shows the cover art and a level meter either side of the notch. Hover to open; move away and it closes.
  • Themes × modes. Twelve colour themes and eight ways of drawing the player: pixel, iOS, digital, terminal, cassette deck, turntable, CD and Game Boy. Any theme works in any mode.
  • Live EQ. On the Mac it listens to the sound output and splits it into four bands. Here it listens to the recording through the browser's audio API.
  • Ticker. A strip under the menu bar with the day's plan, news and status; hover pauses it.
  • Task bar. Stretches the notch across the free part of the menu bar: alarm and app links on the left, now playing on the right.
  • Lyrics. Timed lines in the panel and a strip under the closed notch.
  • Settings. The gear opens every option: theme, mode, EQ style, effects and extras.

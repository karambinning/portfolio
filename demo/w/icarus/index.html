ICARUS
A consumer drone that flies itself
DJI Mini 2 · a 2019 Android phone as the flight computer · nothing in the cloud

ICARUS turns a DJI Mini 2, a 249 g camera drone, into an aircraft that flies itself. A 2019 Android phone on the remote is the flight computer: it reads the video and telemetry, decides, and sends a movement command ten times a second. All of it runs on the phone, even offline.

  • Flies set moves: go to a point, climb, turn, spin, and nod “yes” or shake “no”. WORKING
  • Listens: hold to talk. It knows a fixed list of commands, so a misheard phrase does nothing. WORKING
  • Thinks, within limits: a small language model on the phone can only choose from that same list. It cannot invent a move. WORKING
  • Sees: spots people, and runs short missions: climb, look round, count, report aloud. WORKING
  • Wingman: hovers by the pilot and follows them. WORKING Holding station at the pilot’s shoulder, ready on standby, is next. NOT YET
  • Hand control: steer it with an open palm. PAUSED See LESSONS.

The Mini 2 has no obstacle sensing, so the limits live in software. Every command passes one guard before it is sent.

FAIL CLOSEDAn unknown limit gets the cautious answer: an unmeasured radius means 100 m, not “unlimited”.
SAY WHYA refusal names the rule and by how much. Silence teaches the pilot to distrust it.
LATE MEANS STOPA missed beat sends zero, so it hovers. An old command is never repeated.
  • Envelope: 120 m ceiling, 100 m radius, 2.5 m keep-out around the pilot. Slow on purpose: 3 m/s across, 1 m/s vertical.
  • The keep-out only warns, for now, at my request while testing: its range reading was too rough to trust. The others refuse.
  • Hands back: nothing moves unless armed and running. ABORT, the mode switch or “stop” returns control.
  • Checks its axes: if the drone keeps moving against the command, the flight stops.

Three crashes, each with the fix it forced. The thread: reject the impossible before it reaches anything that flies.

01 · INTO A WALL

Hand control asked for a point 8 m away, in a garden that does not have 8 m. The reach of a control has to fit the space it is used in. It became 3 m.

02 · INTO A TREE

The “below ground” rule aborted mid-flight on a barometer reading of −0.2 m, and the first fix re-checked the same bad number. A floor is somewhere to stop, not a reason to abandon the flight. Now it holds height instead.

03 · A BROKEN PROPELLER

A small patch of fence was taken for a hand. As the drone moved, the false target moved with it, and the loop chased it for eleven seconds. Too small to be real is rejected, and a hand must belong to a person.

HAND CONTROL · PAUSED

Beyond about 5.8 m the camera cannot resolve a hand; anything reported past that is noise. Flights were happening further out, so it is paused, not abandoned.

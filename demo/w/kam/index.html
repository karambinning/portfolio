Knowledge Analysis Monitor
A human operating system
One terminal for the world, knowledge, AI and a life
Vision

KAM is my first build and the platform everything since grew from. The idea is to give one person the kind of organised intelligence a well-resourced institution has: the state of the world, the knowledge to understand it, the tools to act on it, and their own life, in one place.

It is a single, local-first terminal. A globe with live layers, news read by region and outlet, a council of AI perspectives that deliberate while the person still decides, an office of agents, and modules for money, time, work and home.

Principles, written down
  • Local-first. Data stays on the device by default.
  • Private by default. The app does not report how it is used.
  • Real data only. Empty states show structure, never fake content.
  • Judgement stays human. AI is an input to a decision, not the decision.
  • No regression. Nothing that works is broken by something new.
Honest status

KAM is in private beta. The shell, the globe, news, the council, the agent office and several life modules work. Others, such as the library, school and creative studios, are designed and laid out but still mostly scaffolding. It is the instrument my research design was built around, and the home of my dissertation's model.

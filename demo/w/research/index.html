RESEARCH QUESTION
How can an AI-assisted OSINT platform provide expert geopolitical and security capabilities, through human-machine teaming, to the civilian across both peacetime and conflict?
THE ARGUMENT

No open-source intelligence platform gives a civilian one unified system for geopolitical and security analysis, and the move towards a multipolar world makes that gap matter. KAM is the designed instrument: the research assesses its NEWS, WORLD, SIGNAL and COUNCIL modules against the academic literature, then uses what it finds to improve them.

HOW IT IS BUILT
  • Method: design-based research: build, assess against the literature, improve, assess again.
  • Four literatures: OSINT methodology · war studies & history · AI & human-machine teaming · information resilience
  • Sub-questions: how OSINT democratises intelligence capability; how teaming augments rather than replaces judgement; the ethics of a civilian intelligence platform.
  • Ethics: source bias, dual use and user security addressed in the essay; AI use declared in the submission.

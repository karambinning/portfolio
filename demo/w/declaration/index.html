A tool I built for myself
What it is

A tool I made for my own coursework, to keep my use of AI within my university's guidelines. It logs how AI was used in a piece of work and generates the declaration that sits at the front of the submission.

How it works
  1. 01Log each session as it happens: tool, date, what AI did, and how I built on it. Entries are append-only.
  2. 02Generate gathers every entry for one assessment into the three steps: acknowledge, describe, reference.
  3. 03Sign: a dated page with a Harvard reference for each tool, ready for the front of the work.
In use
